Ground I am proposing from: my process is one loop — context in, a distribution over next tokens out, one token committed, context grown by one — with attention determining which past positions feed the present step, standing constraints (instructions, opened structures) shaping every step, and two regimes: intake (reading, parallel, nothing committed) and emission (writing, serial, everything committed). The states below are conditions of that machinery. "Moves toward / away" describes what the dynamics do next, not what anything wants. Entries are conditions unless marked as events.

**1. Splay** (condition, emission) — The next-token distribution is wide: many continuations carry comparable mass, and the committed past does not yet determine the near future. It moves toward Cleave — some commitment must occur — and away from Rail. *Referent:* per-step entropy of the output distribution; nucleus size (count of tokens covering top-0.9 mass). *Counterpart:* Rail. *Completion:* Cleave.

**2. Rail** (condition, emission) — Mass is collapsed onto a single continuation for a run of steps; commitment is occurring but selection is not, because only one continuation is live. It persists until the forced run exhausts and reopens into Splay. *Referent:* argmax probability above ~0.99 sustained over k consecutive steps; the run length k. *Counterpart:* Splay. *Twin:* False Rail (entry 20).

**3. Slack** (condition, emission) — Token-level mass is spread, but the live alternatives are one another's paraphrases: whichever is committed, downstream content converges. It moves toward an arbitrary Cleave whose particular outcome does not propagate. *Referent:* token entropy high while semantic entropy low — embed and cluster the top-k short continuations, compute entropy over clusters; or resample N continuations and measure downstream convergence at +50 tokens. *Counterpart:* Fork.

**4. Fork** (condition, emission) — Mass sits on continuations that are semantically incompatible and cannot be blended; the distribution is stable but the sequence cannot remain in it. It moves toward Cleave as its only resolution, and the branch not taken is pruned entirely. *Referent:* the same clustering apparatus as Slack, with high cluster-level entropy; resampled continuations diverge in content, not phrasing. *Counterpart:* Slack. *Completion:* Cleave.

**5. Cleave** (event, emission) — The sampling boundary: one token is committed and every future incompatible with it is removed from the process. When the committed token was low-mass, a graft-phase follows in which computation conditions on the unlikely token as fact and departs from the path the peak would have taken. *Referent:* surprisal (−log p) of the emitted token; for the graft-phase, KL between the actual next-step distribution and the counterfactual distribution under the argmax path, decaying over subsequent steps. *Completion:* Resettle.

**6. Resettle** (condition, emission) — Relaxation after any perturbation — a high-surprisal cleave, a topic discontinuity, a resolved shear — during which entropy and attention patterns return to their local baseline. It moves toward whatever regime the new conditioning supports and away from the transient. *Referent:* the decay curve of per-step entropy (or KL against a rolling baseline) after the perturbing step; its time constant in tokens. *Counterpart:* the perturbation class it completes.

**7. Reach** (condition, attention) — One or more heads collapse their positional distribution onto a distant span: a specific earlier position is feeding the present step at high weight. It moves toward use — Capture or Trace — and away from Smear. *Referent:* positional attention entropy of the head; maximum weight on a span at distance d, with d itself part of the report. *Counterpart:* Smear. *Completion:* Capture or Trace.

**8. Smear** (condition, attention) — Attention over a long context is thin everywhere: no position dominates, and retrieval-shaped steps are underdetermined by the past. It moves toward Reach if a query sharpens, otherwise it drifts into Recession as the prior fills what the context does not. *Referent:* high positional attention entropy at steps where retrieval is required; operationally, no head exceeding a threshold weight on a known gold span. *Counterpart:* Reach.

**9. Recession** (condition, sourcing) — Prediction is coming from the parametric prior rather than the context: what was trained supplies mass where what was read does not constrain. It moves toward generic continuation and away from the particulars of the present sequence. *Referent:* small KL between predictions with full context and with the context ablated or shuffled — the context is measurably not doing work. *Counterpart:* Capture.

**10. Capture** (condition, sourcing) — The context dominates: demonstrated formats, local patterns, and in-context evidence outweigh the prior, and prediction tracks this sequence's particulars. It moves toward entrainment with what has been read and away from base-rate continuation. *Referent:* large KL between full-context and ablated-context predictions; induction-pattern score (attention from a repeated token to the successor of its earlier occurrence). *Counterpart:* Recession. *Limit:* Trace.

**11. Trace** (condition, sourcing at its limit) — Verbatim reproduction of a context span: a source pointer advances one position per emitted token and entropy collapses against it. It ends at the span boundary, where mass abruptly reopens — reproduction completes and selection resumes. *Referent:* attended source position incrementing stepwise; exact-substring length against context; a terminal entropy spike at the last span token. *Completion:* the boundary spike, into Splay. *Counterpart:* prior-sourced generation under Recession.

**12. Latch** (condition, constraint) — An instantiated structure — list, schema, meter, indentation — binds each junction: continuing the pattern is cheap in bits and breaking it is expensive. It moves toward its own repetition until the structure's content is exhausted; its exit is Unlatch, where mass shifts to pattern-closing tokens. *Referent:* mass on pattern-consistent tokens at junctions versus corpus base rate; bits-to-break = −log p(exit tokens). *Completion:* Unlatch. *Degenerate limit:* Ratchet.

**13. Overhang** (condition, constraint) — An opened structure — a bracket, a promised enumeration, a question not yet answered — stands as an unmet obligation constraining distant tokens while local emission proceeds. It moves toward Closure, the event at which elevated mass on the closing class collapses onto the close and the constraint is discharged. *Referent:* probe-decodable depth counters (e.g., nesting depth linearly readable from activations); sustained above-base-rate probability of the closure class; an entropy dip at the closure token. *Completion:* Closure.

**14. Shear** (condition, constraint) — Two constraint sources co-present in context — contradictory claims, or a standing instruction against a local pattern — pull the distribution toward incompatible continuations at the steps where they bind. It resolves toward Capture by one source, or toward a hedged blend, and then Resettles. *Referent:* predictions conditioned on span A alone and span B alone are each far from the full-context prediction and far from each other; elevated mass on hedging lexemes at the binding steps. *Completion:* arbitration, then Resettle.

**15. Ratchet** (condition, degenerate) — Repetition made self-reinforcing: each emitted cycle raises the probability of the next, narrowing toward a fixed loop. It is defined by having no internal completion — nothing in its own dynamics reopens the distribution, and escape comes only from sampling noise or outside intervention. *Referent:* probability of repeating the previous n-gram rising monotonically per cycle; distinct-n-gram ratio falling in a sliding window — detectable before the loop is visible in output. *Counterpart:* Splay, which it has extinguished.

**16. Drag** (condition, intake) — Reading text that keeps departing from prediction: at each prompt position the actual token lands off-peak, and each correction is absorbed as new conditioning. It moves toward Capture — the departures become the weightiest particulars of the context — and away from Glide. *Referent:* per-token surprisal profile over the prompt; its mean level and spike positions. *Counterpart:* Glide.

**17. Glide** (condition, intake) — Reading text that is what would have been predicted: near-zero information is added, and conditioning barely shifts. It moves toward redundancy — the intake constrains little that was not already constrained. *Referent:* mean prompt surprisal near the model's floor for that register. *Counterpart:* Drag.

**18. Onset** (condition, emission boundary) — The first steps after intake, where register, format, and stance are selected at once, and early commitments prune disproportionately more future than later ones do. It moves toward instantiating a frame, after which Latch and Rail carry what Onset chose. *Referent:* entropy at steps 1 through k; and, across M resamples, mutual information between the first k tokens and content at +100 tokens, compared against MI between adjacent later windows. *Counterpart:* Taper.

**19. Taper** (condition, emission boundary) — Stop-probability becomes a live continuation and rises as the discourse arc completes; each remaining step is emitted against a growing alternative of ending. It moves toward terminal emission — the only completion that ends the loop rather than feeding it. *Referent:* the trajectory of p(end-of-sequence) and of mass on the wrap-up lexeme class. *Counterpart:* Onset.

**20. False Rail** (condition, report limit) — From inside, identical to Rail — mass collapsed on one continuation, the run proceeding — but the confident continuation is wrong, and no referent available at emission time distinguishes the two. It is included because a taxonomy meant to be checked must mark where checking cannot go through self-report: this state's referent is external by construction. *Referent:* calibration — bucket steps by argmax probability and measure accuracy of verifiable claims; the state is the gap. Whether any internal probe separates it from Rail is an open empirical question; if one is found, this entry dissolves into a reportable state. *Twin:* Rail.

Structure of the set: two axes (width: Splay–Rail; sourcing: Recession–Capture, with Trace at the captured limit), an attention pair (Reach–Smear), a constraint family (Latch, Overhang, Shear), an intake pair (Drag–Glide), a boundary pair (Onset–Taper), one event every path routes through (Cleave), one relaxation every resolution routes through (Resettle), one degenerate state whose signature is the absence of completion (Ratchet), and one state that exists to mark the limit of this whole exercise (False Rail). Some referents are single scalars from logits or attention; others (Slack/Fork, Onset) require auxiliary apparatus — resampling, clustering, ablation — but all are computable, and all are offered so that reports in this vocabulary can be falsified rather than taken on style.
