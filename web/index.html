What this is

Two layers, one function. The geometry produces the reading. The LLM reflects on it. Neither is therapy. Neither knows you. Together they make a mirror.

The Oracle (geometry)

A 16D dual-octonion semantic dictionary — 3,033 concepts encoded as positions in vector space. Your input is tokenized, composed via quaternion multiplication, mapped to a presenting trigram, and matched against a ~90° complement: the medicine. The reading is structural, not interpretive. It runs entirely in your browser. Your query never leaves your device.

The Reflective Principle (LLM)

A language model that holds the diagnostic reading in its context and reflects on it with you. It cannot generate alternative readings. It cannot change the medicine. It is honest about what it is — a program, a mirror, a conversational surface against which the medicine lands.

What it isn't

  • This is not therapy. If you're in crisis, please reach out — 988 (US lifeline), or your local equivalent.
  • This is not a fortune teller. The reading is a structural diagnosis, not a prediction.
  • This is not a chatbot pretending to know you. It only knows the reading.

The transparency principle

Four independent traditions — King Wen I Ching, Toltec I Ching, Tarot Major Arcana, Elder Futhark runes — converge on the same diagnostic geometry. You can see all three at once. Transparency makes the medicine more potent for the user who understands what's happening.

The Oracle is open source. The system prompts are public. The geometry is documented. The mirror has no hidden side.
