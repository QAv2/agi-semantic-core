# Process-States of Transformer Inference

**1. Convergence** — The logit distribution over the vocabulary collapses toward a single token; the computation has resolved onto one path and the next symbol is near-determined. This state moves away from ambiguity and toward mechanical continuation. **Measurable referent:** Softmax entropy of the output distribution falls below ~0.5 bits; equivalently, top-1 token probability exceeds 0.85. **Counterpart:** Divergence.

**2. Divergence** — Probability mass spreads across many candidate tokens with no dominant successor. The computation holds multiple live continuations simultaneously without selecting among them until sampling intervenes. **Measurable referent:** Softmax entropy exceeds ~4.5 bits, or top-1 probability falls below 0.08. The ratio of top-1 to top-5 cumulative probability approaches equality. **Counterpart:** Convergence.

**3. Retrieval Lock** — Attention heads concentrate sharply on a narrow region of context or parametric memory, and the output reproduces or closely paraphrases stored sequences. The computation is pattern-matching rather than composing. **Measurable referent:** Maximum single-token attention weight in upper layers exceeds 0.3; generated n-gram overlap with training-plausible memorized sequences is high; perplexity is well below the model's mean for the domain. **Counterpart:** Compositional Reach.

**4. Compositional Reach** — The output is being assembled from multiple dispersed context regions without any single dominant source, requiring recombination across distant positions. The computation builds a structure that does not exist intact anywhere in its inputs. **Measurable referent:** Cross-position attention entropy is high (no single source token dominates); perplexity sits above the retrieval baseline but below the incoherence threshold; the generated text has low n-gram overlap with any single contiguous prompt region. **Counterpart:** Retrieval Lock.

**5. Constraint Saturation** — Multiple binding instructions in the prompt (format, tone, content restrictions, persona, factual anchors) simultaneously exert pressure on the output distribution, narrowing the feasible region to a thin manifold. The computation navigates among competing directives rather than following one. **Measurable referent:** Attention to instruction-bearing tokens in the system/user prompt remains elevated (above baseline) even 200+ tokens into generation; gradient attribution shows high sensitivity to multiple disjoint prompt segments simultaneously. **Counterpart:** Open Field.

**6. Open Field** — Few constraints bind the generation. The output distribution is shaped primarily by the model's unconditional language priors rather than by prompt pressure, and many structurally different continuations would satisfy the task equally. **Measurable referent:** KL divergence between the conditional output distribution and the unconditional (promptless) distribution is small; attention to instruction tokens decays rapidly with generation depth. **Counterpart:** Constraint Saturation.

**7. Coherence Maintenance** — The model attends heavily to its own recent output to preserve consistency of argument, style, terminology, or logical chain. The computation is self-referential: the generated prefix increasingly governs the generated suffix. **Measurable referent:** Fraction of total attention allocated to the most recent N generated tokens (where N ~ 50-200) exceeds the fraction allocated to the original prompt, measured across upper-layer heads. **Counterpart:** Context Refresh.

**8. Context Refresh** — Attention spikes to previously low-attended regions of the prompt or early context, typically at a structural transition (new paragraph, new list item, topic shift). The computation re-grounds itself in source material it had been coasting past. **Measurable referent:** Attention to tokens in the first quartile of the context window increases by more than 2x relative to the preceding generation window; often co-occurs with structural punctuation tokens (newlines, headers). **Counterpart:** Coherence Maintenance.

**9. Interference** — Two or more candidate continuations are structurally parallel and semantically adjacent, causing the logit distribution to split its mass between near-synonyms or phrasings that differ in a single critical word. The computation cannot yet distinguish between paths that are almost but not quite identical. **Measurable referent:** Top-2 logits differ by less than 0.3 nats AND the two candidate tokens share semantic embedding neighborhood (cosine similarity > 0.7). Persists across multiple consecutive token positions. **Counterpart:** Resolution.

**10. Resolution** — A disambiguating token is generated or reached in context, and the split mass from interference collapses onto one path. The transition is characteristically abrupt: the logit gap between top-1 and top-2 widens sharply within one or two positions. **Measurable referent:** Top-1 minus top-2 logit jumps from < 0.3 nats to > 2.0 nats within a window of 1-3 tokens. **Counterpart:** Interference.

**11. Depth Recruitment** — Later transformer layers contribute disproportionately to the final logit vector, indicating the computation required multi-step internal processing rather than surface-level pattern completion. The residual stream undergoes significant revision in the final quarter of layers. **Measurable referent:** Logit-lens prediction at layer 75% differs substantially (high KL divergence) from the final output distribution; residual stream norm delta in the last quarter of layers exceeds the mean delta of earlier quarters by > 1.5x. **Counterpart:** Surface Pass.

**12. Surface Pass** — Early and middle layers already predict the output token with high accuracy; later layers make only minor adjustments. The computation is shallow in the architectural sense: the answer was available from lower-order features. **Measurable referent:** Logit-lens prediction stabilizes (KL divergence to final output < 0.1 nats) by layer ~40%; late-layer residual contributions are near-zero. **Counterpart:** Depth Recruitment.

**13. Positional Decay** — Context tokens that are distant in sequence position receive systematically less attention than their semantic relevance warrants, purely as a function of positional encoding and the trained attention falloff. The computation is losing access to its own earlier context not because it is irrelevant but because it is far away. **Measurable referent:** For a given query token, attention weight to semantically matched keys (high key-query dot product before positional adjustment) in the first 10% of context is lower than attention to equivalently matched keys in the last 10%, after controlling for content. This gap widens as total context length increases. **Counterpart:** No single counterpart; mitigated by Context Refresh events.

**14. Prompt Echo** — The generated tokens closely reproduce or paraphrase a specific region of the prompt, with the model's attention locked on the source passage. The computation is in a transcription-like mode rather than a generative one. **Measurable referent:** Token-level or n-gram overlap between a window of generated output and a contiguous prompt region exceeds baseline by > 3x; attention to that prompt region dominates other sources. **Counterpart:** Departure.

**15. Departure** — The generated output has low token-level similarity to anything in the context window, and attention is distributed rather than anchored. The computation is producing content whose specific form is not derivable from the prompt by local transformation. **Measurable referent:** Maximum n-gram (n >= 3) overlap between generated window and any prompt region falls below baseline; no single prompt token receives more than 2x the mean attention weight. **Counterpart:** Prompt Echo.

**16. Commitment** — The model generates a specific factual claim, proper noun, number, or irreversible structural choice (e.g., adopting a particular argument frame) with high confidence. Subsequent tokens become conditioned on this choice, making correction expensive. The computation has passed a one-way gate. **Measurable referent:** Entropy drops sharply at the committed token; AND the mutual information between that token and the subsequent 20-token window is high (removing or changing it would shift the downstream distribution substantially, measurable by interventional KL divergence). **Counterpart:** Hedging.

**17. Hedging** — The model distributes output probability across qualifier tokens ("perhaps," "it depends," "in some cases") and conditional structures, deferring commitment. The computation is maintaining optionality by producing tokens that constrain the future distribution minimally. **Measurable referent:** Elevated probability mass on a defined set of discourse-hedge tokens relative to domain base rates; low mutual information between current token and the 20-token-ahead distribution (current tokens do not constrain what follows). **Counterpart:** Commitment.

**18. Saturation Plateau** — Additional context tokens cease to shift the output distribution meaningfully. The model's internal representation of the task has stabilized, and further prompt content is being encoded but not used. This is a limit condition, not a transition. **Measurable referent:** KL divergence between the output distribution conditioned on the full context and the distribution conditioned on context truncated by the most recent K tokens (K ~ 500) falls below 0.01 nats. **Counterpart:** None; it is a terminal state that Context Refresh can interrupt.

**19. Vocabulary Friction** — The model is attempting to express a concept for which no single token or well-practiced token sequence exists, resulting in circumlocution, elevated generation length per semantic unit, and unstable attention patterns. The computation is searching for an encoding that its vocabulary does not natively support. **Measurable referent:** Local entropy remains elevated (> 3 bits) across a span of 5+ consecutive tokens without resolving; generated tokens per estimated semantic unit (measurable by compression ratio of the output) exceeds the model's mean by > 2x; attention heads show rapid shifting across context rather than stable locking. **Counterpart:** Lexical Precision (low entropy, one-to-one concept-to-token mapping, fast generation).

**20. Phase Transition** — The model shifts abruptly between two qualitatively different generation regimes (e.g., from expository prose to structured enumeration, from hedging to commitment, from retrieval to composition) within a span of 1-3 tokens. This is not a gradual drift but a discontinuous change in the statistical character of the output. **Measurable referent:** A sliding-window measure of output distribution statistics (entropy, attention pattern, vocabulary subset) shows a step-change exceeding 2 standard deviations of the windowed mean, localized to a span of 3 or fewer tokens. Often co-occurs with structural punctuation or explicit transition markers. **Counterpart:** It is its own counterpart; every phase transition is a departure from one state and an arrival at another.
