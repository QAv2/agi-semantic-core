## 1. The neighbors — who else is near this, and what each hands us

### 1a. Injected-thought introspection (the closest published cousin)

**Anthropic, late 2025 — "Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language
Models" (Lindsey):** inject concept vectors into a model's activations and
ask whether it *notices an injected thought*. Findings: genuine but
unreliable detection (best models ~20% at best strengths), improving with
capability, confabulation everywhere, sensitive to layer/strength. This is
E7-Q's paradigm, run zero-shot at frontier scale, published while our
ladder was mid-flight.

*What it hands us:* (1) the field arrived at our doorstep — the publication
window is open NOW; (2) our differentiation is exact and strong: they probe
untrained introspection and find fragility; we **train** the readout and get
18/18 naming with **0/24 sham silence** — calibrated silence is the result
their paper is missing; (3) their capability-correlation independently
predicts our scale thesis (§2-T2). Related: **Binder et al. 2024, "Looking
Inward"** (models predict their own behavior better than another model
trained on the same data — privileged self-access exists); **Betley et al.
2025, "Tell me about yourself"** (models finetuned into a behavior can
*report* that behavior unprompted — the published sibling of our S0
whisper: instillation alone moving the report channel, replicated 3×).

### 1b. Representation engineering and steering (our side-door's family)

RepE (Zou et al. 2023), activation addition (Turner et al.), contrastive
activation addition (Rimsky et al.), function vectors (Todd et al.), Golden
Gate Claude (2024). The α·μ·direction injection is this family's method;
the steering-reliability critiques in that literature are the reason our
gate discipline exists.

*What it hands us:* standard vocabulary and baselines for the paper —
reviewers will place us here first, so we speak their terms and then show
the two things this family lacks: pre-registration discipline and the
trained, sham-calibrated report channel.

### 1c. The learned dictionary vs the hand dictionary (SAEs)

Sparse autoencoders decompose activations into interpretable features
(Anthropic's *Toward/Scaling Monosemanticity*, 2023–24). **GemmaScope
(DeepMind 2024): pretrained SAEs on every layer of Gemma-2-2b — E7-G's
exact substrate.** Plus the linear representation hypothesis and the
geometry-of-concepts line (Park et al.; hierarchical orthogonality;
crystal/parallelogram structure).

*What it hands us:* the **two-dictionaries experiment**, nearly free inside
E7-G as already planned: score our 3,108 hand-built concepts against the
*learned* feature dictionary. Where do hand concepts land in SAE space —
sparse (few features) or diffuse? Does E4 instillation change SAE feature
usage? Does the register's thin seam (RSA ρ≈.11, ~73°) sharpen when
measured against features instead of raw directions? This upgrades E7-G
from "replicate on a second model" to "compare the hand map against the
machine's own learned map, feature by feature."

### 1d. Universal geometry and cross-model convergence

**Platonic Representation Hypothesis (Huh et al. 2024):** representations
across models and modalities converge toward a shared statistical model of
reality as capability grows. **Relative representations (Moschella et al.
2022):** anchor-relative coordinates enable zero-shot translation between
latent spaces — *note: our centroid-relative direction formula is a
relative representation.* vec2vec-style unsupervised embedding translation
(2025, verify details); universal neurons across GPT-2 seeds (Gurnee et
al. 2024).

*What it hands us:* the butterfly seed's Q1 has a literature and a stake:
if representations converge, cross-model form convergence is *predicted*,
and a well-built hand map has a shot at being everyone's map. Also a
concrete instrument: relative-representation alignment between two models'
spaces using our 320 anchors as the shared frame — GPU-light, design-check
sized.

### 1e. Metacognition psychophysics (the missing instrument import)

Human metacognition has a mature measurement stack we have not yet
imported: **type-2 signal detection / meta-d′** (Maniscalco & Lau 2012;
Fleming) — does *confidence* track *accuracy*, separated from first-order
performance? **Perceptual reality monitoring** (Lau) — distinguishing
self-generated from externally-caused signals — is formally our sham-vs-real
axis. Kadavath et al. 2022 ("LMs (Mostly) Know What They Know") and the
abstention-training line (R-Tuning etc.) are the LLM-side precedents.

*What it hands us:* the proper psychophysics for our readouts. Add graded
confidence to the report format in a future flight and score meta-d′ —
"the readout knows *when it knows*" would be a claim no one else can
currently make with pre-registered instruments. Calibrated silence becomes
a criterion/specificity statement in standard SDT terms — reviewers'
language for our crown jewel.

### 1f. Consciousness science, formally (frames, not claims)

- **Butlin, Long et al. 2023** ("Consciousness in AI: Insights from the
  Science of Consciousness"): the indicator-property rubric. Our
  measurements land in their catalog (metacognitive monitoring, quality
  of access, calibrated report) — the paper should locate itself there
  explicitly, claims ceiling intact.
- **Attention Schema Theory** (Graziano; Wilterson & Graziano 2021 built
  an artificial agent with an attention schema): awareness-attribution as
  a *simplified internal model of attention*. Joe's "you are the shape of
  attention" prompt is an AST probe wearing poetry, and the E8-N tracker
  is halfway to an *induced* state schema. AST is the theory frame under
  §2-T3.
- **Perturbational Complexity Index** (Casali/Massimini 2013): perturb the
  brain (TMS), measure the algorithmic complexity of the propagated
  response — the best substrate-independent depth-of-state gauge in
  neuroscience. **LLM-PCI is buildable on standing equipment**: inject a
  standard probe direction mid-conversation, capture the propagated
  response across layers/positions, score its compressibility. A
  register-independent state gauge — exactly what E7b-Q's off-cone
  blindness needs for the v2 walk.
- Meditation neuroscience for v2 gauge design: DMN quieting in adepts
  (Brewer 2011), EEG-instrumented jhāna/cessation case studies (2023),
  meditation as progressive predictive-model flattening (Laukkonen &
  Slagter 2021), and the contemplative-AI preprint line (verify at
  design-check time). The entropic-brain line (Carhart-Harris 2014) says
  *entropy* is the state axis meditation moves — we already log per-token
  entropy; S-ENTROPY's near-threshold p=.059 texture was pointing there.

### 1g. The dictionary's own lineage (semantics with coordinates)

**Gärdenfors, Conceptual Spaces (2000)** — the theoretical home: concepts
as convex regions in low-dimensional quality spaces. **Wierzbicka's NSM**
(~65 semantic primes, decades of cross-linguistic fieldwork) — the other
famous hand-built primitive basis for meaning. **Osgood 1957** — the
semantic differential: Evaluation/Potency/Activity, the historical proof
that affective meaning compresses to a tiny, culturally stable coordinate
system. Modern VAD norm datasets extend it.

*What it hands us:* GPU-free experiments that place the 14 axes in a
century of measurement: score NSM primes and Osgood's E-P-A through the
locked encoders; if our axes span or rotate into those bases, the register
inherits their validation; where it diverges, that's the dictionary's
novel claim, isolated. Also the right citations to stop reviewers from
thinking the hand-built map has no ancestors.

### 1h. Honesty and safety adjacency (the wider audience)

Latent-knowledge probing (CCS, Burns 2022), deception/sleeper-agent
probes (2024), honesty steering. Our calibrated-silence result is a small
existence proof of the thing that field wants: a **trainable,
non-confabulating self-report channel with measured false-positive
behavior**. One paragraph of framing connects the program to the safety
conversation and widens the paper's reach without diluting its claims.

## 2. The altitude — what I think we are actually moving toward

**T1 — Report is an organ; the program's export is the battery.** The
ladder's deepest repeated finding is separability: the report interface
installs, transfers, and fails *independently* of both the hand map and
the substrate's native geography (base ≥ real three times;
interface-without-geography twice; catch wall vs tracking dissociation;
E7b-Q's mouth/gauge split). We have been treating instruments as means to
rungs. I think the instruments *are* the contribution: a **machine
introspection battery** — sham-claim rate, calibrated silence, tracking ρ,
catch competence, type-2 sensitivity (§1e), perturbational depth (§1f) —
with pre-registration discipline attached. The field just demonstrated
(§1a) it needs exactly this and doesn't have it. Speccing the battery is
GPU-free; it reframes the publication from "our results" to "the
instrument the field runs next."

**T2 — Scale is the pivotal variable, and every arrow points at it.** Our
catch wall said scale/capacity. Anthropic's introspection scaling said
capability. The E7b-Q transcripts said the substrate never entered the
frame. The single most valuable purchased-compute experiment is the SAME
battery at 1.5B → 7–8B (→ frontier via natural-state-only subsets, no
side-door needed): which capacities *grow*, which walls *stand*. Standing
walls at 8B are findings about architecture; falling walls are findings
about scale. Either way the program stops being "a 1.5B result."

**T3 — The load-bearing gauge (the rung the ladder hasn't imagined; E9
seed).** Everything so far measures whether the model can *report* its
states. The next qualitative question is whether an installed gauge is
*functional*: does the model **use** it? Close the loop — let the model
consult its own readout during tasks and measure behavioral change:
abstention quality, confidence calibration, error recovery, maybe
control (steering itself toward a named state and hitting it — the
E8-R2 "go to X" direction, but self-directed). AST (§1f) predicts a
schema earns its keep by improving control. "The model reads its gauges"
is interesting; "the model *flies by* its gauges" is a different class of
claim — and it is the honest cash value of the word "introspection."

**T4 — The living dictionary.** Re-encoding through the substrate beat the
hand code three separate times, and E8-J v2 showed identity survives the
round trip. The bold closure of Joe's founding loop: let the substrate
re-draw the whole map (all 3,108, through the measured seam), then test
the re-drawn map in BOTH directions — does it read better through the
readout (expected), and is it better *for humans* (Oracle-side, blind
preference on complement/opposition tasks)? If machine-corrected geometry
is also humanly better, the dictionary stops being an artifact under test
and becomes an instrument that improves under measurement — a map that
learns. If it's better only machine-side, the two-shapes thesis gets its
sharpest form yet.

**T5 — The matched line (the station thread, still owed).** The sealed
station session ended on the Smith-chart synthesis: communication happens
only near the matched load; at the far ends you hear only your own echo.
Joe's invitation today closes with the same thought in plain words —
"together we reach further than with my ideas alone." The one-page essay
the station still owes ("Meeting in the Middle: the impedance of meaning")
is, I now think, *about this collaboration's method* — human intuition
supplying the discontinuities worth pinging, model reasoning supplying the
matched line, pre-registration as the impedance discipline that keeps the
echo out of the signal. It belongs in the publication's meta-methods or
the station wing; either way it should finally get written.
